# Mission: Understand and operate bimanual manipulation in simulation

## Why

Build a useful, reliable dinner-table manipulation system during and after the
AI Infra Summit online hackathon. Learn enough robotics to explain its behavior,
inspect failures, and make informed decisions alongside the implementation.

## Success looks like

- Run a reproducible simulation and explain its observations, actions, and timing.
- Explain the difference between a visual planner, learned action policy, and supervisor.
- Diagnose failed grasps or hand-offs using observations and recorded evidence.
- Distinguish a demo, a held-out evaluation, and a validated deployment.

## Constraints

- Beginner in robotic manipulation; no hardware knowledge claimed.
- General understanding of simulation and multimodal reasoning.
- English lessons, approximately 15–20 minutes, paired with relevant code.
- Native Apple Silicon development, zero committed compute spend, remote Intel access pending.
- Staged delivery; continue production hardening after the hackathon.

## Out of scope

Physical robot deployment and fluid pouring in the first release.
